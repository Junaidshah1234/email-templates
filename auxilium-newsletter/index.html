What’s Changing Across the Region
Saudi Arabia
Saudi Arabia has introduced a 70% Saudisation requirement for selected project management professions, effective 14 February 2027.
UAE
Companies subject to Emiratisation requirements are entering the final months of H2 2026, ahead of the 31 December compliance deadline.
Bahrain
A new digital service has been introduced to simplify work-permit renewals for eligible Golden Residency holders.
Kuwait
From 1 September 2026, changes apply to automatic extensions for visit visas and absence and leave permits.
